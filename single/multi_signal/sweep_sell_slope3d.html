52 symbols, 9×8×12 = 864 cells, 45.4 min. One panel per PERCENT_SELL; inside a panel, rows are the slope window and columns are k. The colour scale is SHARED across panels, so a panel that looks pale is pale.
k is normalised: SELL_MIN_SIGNAL1 = k / sqrt(window). The slope's spread goes as w-0.494, so a raw threshold would mean something different in every row and the cube would be measuring the normalisation.
Read Symbols moved and Up minus down before Paired median. The arm makes about 2.35 sells per symbol in fifteen years, so most parameter moves leave most symbols bit-identical and the paired median sits on exactly 0.00 almost everywhere. A block with high Symbols moved and consistently positive Up minus down is a real plateau even when every median in it reads zero.
864 cells means about 43 of them clear p<0.05 by chance. Never believe a single best cell; check Neighbours agreeing, which counts how many of a cell's 26 three-dimensional neighbours also beat live on the sign test.
Panels 30, 40, 50 run a different mechanism. PERCENT_BUY = 60, so below that the sell zone sits inside the buy zone, both conditions are true on the same bar, and the churn guard starts biting — the buy arm stands down wherever the sell fires. Those panels are outlined and are not on the same footing as the rest.
