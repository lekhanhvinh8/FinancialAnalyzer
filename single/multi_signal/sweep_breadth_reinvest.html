Click any row to make it the reference — the vs column recomputes against it from the per-symbol numbers. That is the only way to compare two settings: subtracting two vs values would be median(A−ref) − median(B−ref), the same mistake one level up.
median, win and worst must never be subtracted between rows: the symbol at the median of one column is a different symbol from the one at the median of another. mean is the exception and may be subtracted — it is linear. vote is a pairwise round robin, one vote per symbol.
Green marks the best cell in each column. mean is hostage to the largest symbol, vote is blind to size. buys / sells are the mechanism check: if they barely move across the grid, this threshold is not what decides.
