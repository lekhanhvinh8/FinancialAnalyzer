PERCENT_REINVEST_TOP sweep

52 symbols, 20 settings plus None, 2.7 min. One parameter only — the gates and everything else stay at their configured values, and the median trigger is switched off in every row, so this measures the top trigger on its own and PERCENT_REINVEST never enters a figure.
The condition is (price − high)/high × 100 ≤ PERCENT_REINVEST_TOP against the 1000-bar high. The quantity is always ≤ 0, so thresholds are negative and −30 reads as “at least 30% off the high”. More negative is stricter and redeploys less — the same direction as PERCENT_REINVEST, since both fire while dev is below the threshold.
None is no trigger at all; with the median one off that means the arm never redeploys, and it drops the 400-bar timeout with it because that lives on the same class. It is a low floor and every cell clears it easily — read the context line under the table, not the vs column alone.

Click any row to make it the reference — the vs column recomputes against it from the per-symbol numbers. That is the only way to compare two settings: subtracting two vs values would be median(A−ref) − median(B−ref), the same mistake one level up.
median, win and worst must never be subtracted between rows — the symbol at the median of one column is a different symbol from the one at the median of another. mean is the exception and may be subtracted: it is linear. vote is already a pairwise round robin.
reinv turns amber past 10 per symbol, against the live condition's 3.96. A second trigger can look good purely by redeploying constantly, so read it before the vs column.
