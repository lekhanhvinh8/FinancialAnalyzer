52 symbols, 18 settings plus None, 2.7 min. One parameter only — the gates and everything else stay at their configured values, and the median trigger is switched off in every row, so this measures the bottom trigger on its own and PERCENT_SELL never enters a figure.
The condition is (price − low)/low × 100 > PERCENT_SELL_BOTTOM against the 1000-bar low. Larger is stricter and sells less, the same direction as PERCENT_SELL. None is no trigger at all, which with the median one off means the arm never sells.
Scale note: the low is a floor, so distance to it is one-sided and large — the median day sits +132% above it. A number that looks enormous beside PERCENT_SELL is not necessarily loose.

median, win and worst must never be subtracted between rows — the symbol at the median of one column is a different symbol from the one at the median of another. mean is the exception and may be subtracted: it is linear. vote is already a pairwise round robin.
