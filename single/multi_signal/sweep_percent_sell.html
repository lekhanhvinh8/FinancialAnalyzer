The symbol at the median of one column is a different symbol from the one at the median of another, because changing the threshold reorders the ranking — on this grid that mistake reads +5.51 for a cell that is worse for 33 of 48 symbols. mean is the exception and may be subtracted: it is linear, so mean(A)−mean(B) is exactly mean(A−B). vote is already a pairwise round robin.
