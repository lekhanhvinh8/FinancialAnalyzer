Sell threshold × reinvest threshold

52 symbols, 7×10 = 70 cells, 4.6 min. Everything else stays at its configured value; only these two move, and neither of them touches the warmup, so every cell is judged on identical bars.
Why this pair. The sell arm releases at PERCENT_SELL above the median and the reinvest arm may only return within PERCENT_REINVEST of it, so the gap between them is a zone the money cannot come back through. Live that gap is 75 − 10 = 65 points, and on a compounder the price never closes it: FPT took 538 to 1101 bars to fall back within +10% of its median, while REINVEST_TIMEOUT_DAYS is 400. Five of the seven FPT and GMD re-entries are that timeout firing, each buying back higher than it sold.
Live cell: PERCENT_SELL=75, PERCENT_REINVEST=10 — every paired figure is measured against it.
